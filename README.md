# Tapformat

Convert any file without leaving Finder. Select a file, tap **⇧⌘**, pick a format.

**[Download Tapformat for Mac](https://github.com/smit-vanani/tapformat-releases/releases/latest/download/Tapformat.dmg)** · macOS 15 or later · Apple silicon & Intel · [tapformat.com](https://tapformat.com)

## Installing

1. Open `Tapformat.dmg` and drag **Tapformat** into **Applications**.
2. Open Tapformat. macOS says it can't verify the app, because Tapformat isn't registered with Apple's paid developer program yet. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to *"Tapformat was blocked…"* and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.

You only do this once. Updates install automatically after that.

## What it does

- **Convert**: images to JPG, PNG or PDF; PDFs to JPG or PNG; documents to PDF or Word; audio to MP3, M4A or WAV; video to MP4, MOV, GIF or MP3.
- **Shrink** photos, PDFs and videos, keeping the format.
- **Crop** (1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 9:16, auto-trim), **Resize**, **Rotate**, **Mute** videos, and merge PDFs.
- Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

Tapformat includes [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) (LGPL v2.1), built from the official sources, for MP3, GIF and MKV/WebM support.
